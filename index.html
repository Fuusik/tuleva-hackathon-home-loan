Understand repayment schedules

Annuity: a level payment while the interest rate stays the same. Useful when you want a lower initial payment.

Equal principal: a higher initial payment that falls as the balance decreases. At the same amount, term and constant rate, total interest is lower.

Changing interest rates can change both schedules. Your preference is not a bank commitment.
