Compare fictional offers

After the local application simulation, load fictional offers or enter demo offer terms manually. These are not bank quotes. Unknown costs stay unknown; 0 means an explicitly absent cost.

This scenario assumes a constant benchmark, margin and entered costs, with a six-month rate reset. Different amounts, terms, currencies, benchmark/reset periods and special schedules are not combined. Rates and insurance costs can change.

Cost over the chosen horizon means interest plus entered fees and recurring costs. Repaid principal is excluded; remaining principal is shown separately. Full-term outflow includes repaid principal.
