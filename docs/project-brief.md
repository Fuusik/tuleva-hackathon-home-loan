# Project brief

Last updated: 10 October 2026, v6.

## Problem and audience

Estonian consumers repeat information and document entry across banks and face offers with different financing amounts, stages, conditions and costs. The product concept is one checked dataset, visible recipient-specific gaps, transparent comparison and one reviewed final-offer request before personal handoff. Estonian is the default; English is available.

Value is framed around repeated work, initial cash needs, monthly payment burden, adequate financing and conditions. Time savings, willingness to pay and service impact have not been measured. No guaranteed or representative savings, agreed price, bank/Tuleva partnership or accepted submission channel is established.

## Implemented scope

- One continuous application: Loan goal, Applicants, Home and financing, Documents, Review. No separate goal/data/source/review modes or question-by-question navigation gates. A completion count and linked/inline errors support editing.
- Purpose-driven questions and a purchase home-search branch. Missing property/valuation information remains visible as later-stage bank research gaps; the demo does not establish acceptance of an initial request.
- Independent fictional primary/co-applicant profiles, repeated income/debt/limit rows, shared expenses, selected-bank extras, construction/refinancing/guarantee/collateral branches.
- Calculated purchase price/budget from loan plus cash contribution and any explicit MES example funding. Collateral and guarantees are not cash. Annuity/equal-principal explanations with entered-amount examples and an ask-for-both option.
- Compact potential-source guidance plus blank-only synthetic prefill, with current-value provenance. Identity alone is not financial access; contract terms, classifications and acknowledgements remain manual.
- One owner-separated document section with original files and optional per-person/per-bank EMTA choices. All data/files remain in page memory.
- Banks selected at review. Shared and per-person recipient acknowledgements precede one-button local simulation for all selected banks. Six illustrative layouts preserve missing/unverified topics; LHV/Luminor are deeper partial mappings, the other four preparation summaries.
- Offer continuation after the current application is prepared. Generic fictional examples and manual demo intake only; historical household cases are removed. Summary cards precede expandable assumptions, evidence, costs and version details.
- Monthly/initial/horizon budget comparison, one reviewed final-offer round, manual replies, personal handoff and manual contract checks. The existing reviewed JSON/ZIP package remains available.

## Boundaries and verification

Buildless/offline, no installation or paid API. No authentication, registry/bank access, parsing, uploads, sending, live eligibility decisions, genuine consent, persistence or analytics. Reload discards entries/files. Application edits invalidate preparation; offer/disclosure/scenario edits invalidate relevant negotiation/export review. A completed demo is not a complete bank application.

Constant-rate EUR annuity/equal-principal calculations only. Hypothetical normalisation is separate from original financing; incompatible schedules/currencies/reset periods withhold common savings calculations. Unknown costs stay unknown; complete totals/payback are withheld. First equal-principal payment is not an ongoing fixed monthly amount. No prediction of future rates or legal verification is made.

The owner accepted changes 1–12 from the agreed usability list, removed historical examples and excluded points 13 and 14. No new draft save/import feature or user/mobile/accessibility research programme is implemented. Routine code verification remains necessary. There are 90 passing Node tests; browser interaction and visual layout could not be verified in this environment.

## Future decisions

Full bank specifications, accepted channels/file rules, genuine applicant/co-applicant control, permitted source integrations, regulatory/security/retention design, offer parsing and variable-rate modelling remain unresolved production work. Pricing and hosting are not agreed.
